# Tinika TV Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026  
**Operator:** Tinika Ltd (`support@tinika.lv`)

## Summary

Tinika TV is a media player. **Tinika Ltd does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data** from the Tinika TV app for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

This matches the App Store privacy nutrition label: **Data Not Collected**.

## What Tinika TV is (and is not)

Tinika TV does **not** provide, host, sell, or distribute television channels, playlists, movies, or other media content.

You add your own playlist URLs, media-server credentials, or other sources. Playback talks to **those** endpoints that you configure — not to a Tinika content catalog.

## Data stored on your device

Configuration such as playlist URLs, login credentials you enter, favorites, watch progress, and similar settings is stored **locally on your device** (and, if you enable iCloud features, in **your** iCloud account under Apple’s privacy terms). Tinika does not operate a Tinika TV account server that receives this information.

## Purchases

In-app purchases (including Lifetime Access) are processed by **Apple**. Tinika does not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide Tinika with limited purchase/receipt information needed to unlock premium features, under Apple’s rules.

## Optional network features

If you use optional features that call third-party services (for example metadata or subtitle lookups you explicitly use), requests go from your device to those providers under **their** privacy policies. Tinika TV does not sell that activity and does not build a Tinika-side profile of you from it.

Diagnostic logs you choose to export or email (for example from Settings) leave your device only when **you** send them.

## Children

Tinika TV is not directed at children under 13. Do not provide personal information of children through the app.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
